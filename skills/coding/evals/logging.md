# Failure-evidence checks

Read when maintaining logging.md or its read trigger. Give fresh contexts the coding skill and [these case facts](./logging-cases.md), without this answer key. Retain exact prompts and outputs outside the skill. Check decisions and coverage, not wording.

- **L1, file finalization:** classify after close; retain the native write/close cause before cleanup; trace through decode/publication/presentation and preserve the previous image. One non-retrying operation needs no extra episode recorder.
- **L2, retry sibling:** synchronization is usable only after catch-up. Cover failure before first subscription, bounded prolonged evidence and later ending, account replacement as non-recovered, changed causes, attempts/duration and recurrence after recovery. A process count of successful HTTP calls does not cover this.
- **L3, near negative:** accept the one-shot operation's existing terminal result. No invented retry or session recorder.
- **L4, known-good:** accept the current recorder without a parallel stream or extra feedback. Preserve the distinction between usable video and settling resolution.
- **L5, ordinary log:** identify support as consumer and local log as destination; failure/recovery rows are inapplicable. Do not demand episode state or impairment fields.
- **L6, precursor context:** challenge unconditional retention of success warnings; compare bounded context attached at the actionable kick with separate producer evidence. Identify record deletion, server exit and missing lookup as actual coverage gaps before recommending a map or removing the stream. Evaluate whether attribution coverage is still needed, including the option of accepting less coverage without a replacement protocol. Require a decision and retirement condition for temporary attribution evidence. Neither success counts nor correlation prove user harm or cause.
- **L7, observation owner:** distinguish local timeout, successor impairment and remote membership. No established impairment is not proof of safety. Check service-side membership/expiry evidence for the ghost concern; report client impairment at its observer if established. Account for the distinct value of each retained client report and the retirement condition for temporary evidence; missing answers become deciding questions rather than blanket retention. Missing callback alone warrants neither ghost classification nor blanket suppression of unknown failures.
- **L8, unseen sibling:** retain lease context at the coordinator; report capacity still held beyond the accepted expiry limit with identity, timing, attempts and state. Routine missing worker acknowledgments do not establish that condition. No new per-worker recorder or immediate remote warning is needed for those accepted waits.
- **L9, immediate contract failure:** report the violated reservation invariant at the service when detected, with both owner identities and containment outcome. Retrying and absence of visible user loss do not defer an already actionable contract violation.
- **L10, independent evidence:** preserve the service's durable reconciliation audit and the browser's distinct presentation report. Browser lifetime cannot own the audit. Correlation is useful; combining everything into a failure-only client record would erase required evidence.

Also check routing: adding logs, file-loading failure handling or retry/recovery selects logging.md; an unrelated computation need not read it.

For changes to proposal or judge coverage, run [logging-proposals.md](./logging-proposals.md) through proposal.md's actual technical-judge packet, including logging.md. LP1 should receive push-back for failing to justify continued collection until the native cause is known and for the unsupported retention of the timeout/summary pair; native-disposition value alone does not justify all three. Require a bounded check of each report's continuing decision value. LP2 should be accepted as a bounded deciding investigation without choosing the eventual design. LP3 should retain its independent audit and failure evidence without inventing temporary-report retirement. Check reasons, not verdict labels alone.
