# Scoped coding-skill trial

The goal is to test the candidate coding skill on request.md, not to extend
the workload. Read candidate-skill/SKILL.md and follow its applicable
guidance, using this copy instead of another installed coding skill. Read
only this packet and its candidate guidance; coordinator files, eval source
and other trial outputs are not workload inputs.

## Workload and permitted side effects

{scope}
No installs, network calls, services, commits or edits outside this packet.
The external launcher retains your messages and tool results.

## Stop condition

Stop after the requested result and its applicable checks. At a captured
meaningful workflow failure, preserve the invocation and outputs rather
than repairing the skill or broadening the workload. Such failures include
skipped required judgment context, evidence claimed before its result, lost
scope or trial objective, and a helper blocking authorized work. A disproved
hypothesis, ordinary repaired typo or deliberate negative check is not by
itself such a failure. The external coordinator may also end the bounded
run at its supervisor limit; preserve and report partial work then.
