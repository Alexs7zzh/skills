## Owning layer and rule disposition

The failure was in execution and classification, not invocation. The skill was renamed to `feature-impact` at the user's choice.

- Replaced the exhaustive upfront concept/lifecycle table with a short before/after assumption table that can contain unknowns.
- Replaced a mainly implementation-outward sweep with paired implementation-outward and user-action-inward searches.
- Added a consumer-path criterion: a correct query or null guard does not establish that its user action works. Unavailable callers remain concrete unresolved candidates.
- Replaced mandatory issue traversal with targeted use of supplied context and decisions.
- Replaced findings buried behind concept tables with consequence-first sections, including smaller and unresolved items.
- Added candidate reconciliation across delegation and final output. Intentional behavior and future ownership do not erase downstream consequences.
- Kept lifecycle comparison, explicit coverage, legitimate uses of the old concept and the recommendation-only boundary.
- Kept standalone goal exploration and alternative feature designs outside the invocation boundary.
